# 21-day roadmap · Nov 1–21, 2026

> [!important] Exam: Sun 22 November
> **Nov 1 = setup. Nov 2–21 = 20 study days.** The schedule is deliberately *selective*: two targeted slices on shorter days and three focused subjects on Monday–Wednesday, NOT full mastery of 92 chapters. It also reserves board-writing practice every regular day.

## Study intensity by weekday

| Weekday | Target active time | Your plan |
|:---|---:|:---|
| **Monday** | **4h 20m** | 3 subjects + board writing + 18 timed CAET questions |
| **Tuesday** | **4h 20m** | 3 subjects + board writing + 18 timed CAET questions |
| **Wednesday** | **4h 20m** | 3 subjects + board writing + 18 timed CAET questions |
| Thursday and Friday | 2h 35m | 2 subjects, 12–20 timed questions, boards and review |
| Saturday and Sunday | Usually 2h 35m | Lighter/buffer tasks **except scheduled full mocks** |
| Nov 21 | 60–90m | Light formula review and exam-day readiness |

> [!tip] Extra-time days are flexible, not compulsory
> On Monday–Wednesday, your first two study blocks are the **core plan**. If you have ~2h35m rather than 4h20m, skip the third-subject extension and shorten the timed set. Never sacrifice sleep to complete checkboxes. Take breaks outside the active study total.

### Monday–Wednesday · extended blocks

| Block | Active time | Evidence of completion |
|---|---:|---|
| Focus A | 60 min | Concept recall + 8–10 reviewed problems |
| Focus B | 60 min | Concept recall + 8–10 reviewed problems |
| Third-subject extension | 50 min | 8–10 questions / mini reaction map |
| CAET timed mixed set | 40 min | 18 questions with marked guesses and accuracy |
| Boards written | 35 min | Fully written solutions; check marking scheme |
| Error review | 15 min | Correct three errors, update strength |

### Other regular days · standard blocks

45 min Focus A + 45 min Focus B + 30 min timed practice + 25 min boards + 10 min review = **2h35m active**. Mock test days are exceptions; follow the paper length and allow error review. If overwhelmed, choose just one focus block and five questions.

## A crucial distinction

- **Class 12 board chapters not in this short calendar** are still in the [[Checklist Hub]]. Schedule them after CAET or swap them in if you already know a scheduled topic.
- **Class 11 + CAET extras** can be chosen if your mocks repeatedly show that gap; don't force all of them into 20 days.
- **English and an extra board subject:** 20-minute slots are assigned on buffer days; add additional practice according to your school calendar.
- **For your board-specific chapters**, use the official current syllabus, especially if not CBSE.
