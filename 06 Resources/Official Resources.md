# 🔗 Official resources and important dates

## Verified CAET 2027 dates

- **Sun, 22 November 2026** — Session 1 examination.
- **Sat, 14 November 2026, 5:00 PM UAE time** — Session 1 registration deadline.
- **19–22 November 2026** — published test-card download window; check your official account.
- CAET allows **up to two sessions** from four in the 2027 cycle, with the better normalized score considered (subject to published rules).

The official CAET prep FAQ describes **2026 Session 1** as a three-hour, 60-question, 240-mark paper. That is a useful historical mock benchmark, **not a promise** that 2027 Session 1 will use the same format or marking. Read your own test instructions.
