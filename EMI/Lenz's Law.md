Q. Why is Lenz's Law a statement of conservation of energy?

```
When a north pole is moved towards a coil, a current should be induced such that it produces a north pole to oppose the change in flux.

Lets imagine the contrary - a south pole is created.
In this case, the magnet will be attracted towards the coil with an increasing acceleration and the velocity and KE will continously increase without expending any energy. 
Therefore creating a perpetual movng machine, violating the law of conservation of energy. 

Hence, a north pole must be formed in a coil when a north pole is brough towards the coil.
```
