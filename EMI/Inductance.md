#Definitions #EMI 
It is the property of a material to oppose any change in current in a system is called Inductance.

For closely wound coil of N turns
$$\begin{align}
N \frac{d\phi}{dt}&\propto \frac{dI}{dt}\\
Nd\phi &\propto dI\\
\\
Nd\theta&=kdI
\end{align}$$
k - Coefficient of inductance
Unit - Henry
