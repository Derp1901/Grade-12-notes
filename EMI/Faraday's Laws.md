#Definitions 
Whenever the magnetic flux related to a coil change, an EMF is induced in it. The magnitude of the induced EMF is equal to the rate of change of magnetic flux in the circuit.

$$E=\frac{dΦ}{dt}$$
For N no. of loops
$$E=N\frac{dΦ}{dt}$$
Induced current flowing through the coil
$$I=\frac{E}{R}=\frac{N}{R}\frac{dΦ}{dt}$$
Charge flowing due to the induced current
$$q=Idt=\frac{N}{R}{dΦ}$$
