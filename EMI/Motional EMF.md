#Derivation #EMI 
Consider a magnetic field 'B' acting perpendicular to a rectangular coil of length 'l' and breadth 'x'. The region PQ is movable along the rectangle.
![[Pasted image 20260905221839.png|395]]

$$\begin{align}
\phi&=BA\cos\theta\\
&=Blx\cos{0}\\ \\
\phi&=Blx
\end{align}$$
When PQ moves with a velocity 'v', 'x' changes, ie area changes
$$\begin{align}
E&=\frac{d\phi}{dt}\\
&=\frac{d}{dt}Blx\\
&=Bl \frac{dx}{dt}\\
E&=Blv
\end{align}$$

Let B make an angle θ with the direction of velocity vector then, 
$$E=Blv\sin\theta$$
