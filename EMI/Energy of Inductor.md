#Derivation #EMI 
Consider a inductor with self inductance L. A work needs to be done against the back emf in establishing the current, the work done is stored as magnetic potential energy.

$$\begin{align}
P&=VI\\
\frac{d\omega}{dt}&=EI\\
\frac{d\omega}{dt}&=L \frac{dI}{dt} .I\\
d\omega&=LIdI\\ \\
\omega &=\frac{1}{2}LI^2
\end{align}$$
