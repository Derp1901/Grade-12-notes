#Definitions
Property of a coil by virtue of which the coil opposes any change in the strength of current flowing through it by inducing en EMF in itself is called Self Inductance

#Point #EMI
Self inductance is the inertia of electric system.

#Derivation #EMI 
Consider a coil of N turns connected to an AC supply, then the flux linkage is $\propto$ current.
$$\begin{align}
N\phi &\propto I\\
\\
N\phi&=LI
\end{align}$$
L - Coefficient of Self Inductance
L = Amount of magnetic flux when an unit current flows through
