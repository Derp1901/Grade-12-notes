## Definition
The amount of magnetic field lines passing through a unit area.

Represented by 

Consider a Magnetic field 'B', an area 'A' placed in B such that the normal makes an angle θ
with the B.

Φ = BAcosθ

Φ = vector B . vector A

Dimensions - $$ML^2T^2 A^{-1}$$
Unit -
	SI = Weber (Wb)
	CGS = Maxwell (mx)
