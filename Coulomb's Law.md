The force between the two charges is proportional to the product of charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them.
$$\begin{align}
F&\propto q_{1}q_{2}\\
F&\propto \frac{1}{r^2}\\ \\
F&=\frac{kq_{1}q_{2}}{r^{2}} = \frac{1}{4\pi \epsilon_{o}} \frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r^2}
\end{align}$$
