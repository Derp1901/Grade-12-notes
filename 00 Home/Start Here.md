# Start here · 5-minute setup

> [!important] The simplest workflow
> **Open [[Dashboard]] → open today's note → do today's checkboxes → visit [[Checklist Hub]] to rate your chapters.** That's it.

## Step 1 · Open the vault

1. Unzip the download.
2. Open **Obsidian → Open folder as vault** → choose `CAET-Boards-21-Day-Obsidian`.
3. Pin [[Dashboard]] and [[Checklist Hub]] as favourite tabs.

## Step 2 · Activate the interactive controls

Go to **Settings → Community plugins → Browse** and install the plugins from Obsidian's own community listing:

| Plugin | Use | Necessary? |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta Bind** | Change chapter strength, revision switch and mock scores directly | **Yes for interactive ratings** |
| **Dataview** | Auto-updating strength statistics and weak-topic lists | **Yes for dashboards** |
| **Tasks** | Dashboard showing today's and overdue checklist tasks | **Recommended** |
| **Calendar** | Open each date easily | Optional |

> [!warning] Plugin note
> Plugins are not bundled. You choose whether to install them. The Markdown day checklists and chapter notes work without any plugin. Only enable community plugins you trust.

**If you enable Calendar:** turn on Obsidian's core **Daily notes** feature and set folder `02 Daily Plan`, date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Step 3 · Make it look good

The `caet-focus.css` theme snippet is included; it should be pre-enabled when Obsidian reads the vault's appearance setting. Otherwise go to **Settings → Appearance → CSS snippets → caet-focus** and enable it. You can disable it whenever you like.

## Step 4 · Rate before studying

Open [[Checklist Hub]] → [[Physics Checklist]], [[Chemistry Checklist]] or [[Mathematics Checklist]]. You can now use strength dropdowns **directly in the checklist**, no need to enter 92 chapter pages just to rate them.

- **Not studied** = brand new; **Weak** = need help; **Okay** = routine questions possible; **Strong** = reliable on unseen timed questions.
- Toggle **Revised?** only *after* the session and mistakes review. Reviewed does not imply Strong.
- The notes' five-step practice checklists are optional guidance and can be reused.

## Step 5 · Study today, not every day at once

[[21-Day Roadmap]] now links to dated notes with **exact subtopics, question counts, a board-written task and a success checkpoint**. **Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays now have 4h20m extended plans** (3 subjects, 18 timed CAET questions, 35 minutes of written board practice). Other regular days stay around 2h35m. The full mocks are longer exceptions. Every day includes a lower-energy alternative.

**Date clarity:** 1 Nov is setup, 2–21 Nov are 20 study days, 22 Nov is the scheduled CAET Session 1 date. This vault assumes CBSE for boards; adapt chapter priorities if you're on another board.

> [!tip] Your high-capacity days
> **Nov 2–4 · Nov 9–11 · Nov 16–18**. Open these dated daily notes first if you want to see exactly how the extra time is used.
