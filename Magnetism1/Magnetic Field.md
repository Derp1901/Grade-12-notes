#Definitions #Mag1
Magnetic field is the space or region around a magnet where magnetic force is experienced.

#Definitions #Mag1 
#### Magnetic field intensity (B)
It represents the strength of magnetic field at a particular point.

It is a vector quantity 
SI Unit - Tesla(T)
