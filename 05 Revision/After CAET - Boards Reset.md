# 📖 After CAET — switch fully to boards

CAET Session 1 is **22 November 2026**. Class 12 boards remain a separate goal; don't assume an entrance-test score replaces board requirements.

## The first two days after CAET

- [ ] Take time to recover from the exam.
- [ ] Check the **official board timetable** and list your remaining subjects and test dates.
- [ ] For each Class 12 chapter, revisit any unfinished board written-answer practice in [[Chapter Explorer]].
- [ ] Create a board-only revision timetable based on the actual exam dates and your current strengths.

## Weekly boards cycle (repeat until your first paper)

| Day type | Activity |
|---|---|
| 3 days/week | One long chapter with textbook, written questions and marking-scheme review |
| 2 days/week | Timed short questions, examples and weak-chapter recall |
| 1 day/week | Previous/sample board paper + analyse errors |
| 1 flexible day | Other subjects / catch-up / genuine rest |

**Board-only topics to prioritize after CAET:** [[P12 - Semiconductor Electronics]], [[C12 - Biomolecules]], [[C12 - Coordination Compounds]], [[C12 - d- and f-Block Elements]], [[M12 - Differential Equations]], [[M12 - Linear Programming]].

Remember English and your optional subject(s) too—this vault doesn't presume what those are.
